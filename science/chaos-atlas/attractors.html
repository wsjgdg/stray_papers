FIELD NOTE / 0202 — 04

Stretch, fold, repeat.

Nearby states can stretch apart even as a volume of phase space contracts. In a bounded attractor, folding brings those stretched regions back together.

∇ · f = −σ − 1 − β

For Lorenz, the divergence is constant and negative.

The divergence depends on the selected system and its parameters; for Rössler it also varies with position.

FIELD NOTE / 0303 — 04

Measuring separation.

An unseen probe is repeatedly rescaled to stay close to path A. Its accumulated growth gives a running finite-time estimate.

Current estimate / time unit—
λ̂ = Σ ln(dᵢ / d₀) / Σ Δtᵢ

This is a finite-time, single-direction estimate, not an exact maximal exponent. A positive short-run value alone does not prove chaos.

Lorenz stability boundaries

For σ = 10 and β = 8/3: below ρ = 1 the origin is locally stable; between 1 and approximately 24.74 the two nonzero equilibria are locally stable; above 24.74 they are unstable. This Hopf boundary is not a universal chaos switch: a chaotic attractor and stable equilibria can coexist for some lower values of ρ.

Each path uses fixed-step fourth-order Runge–Kutta. In a chaotic regime, numerical errors eventually lead to different individual trajectories.
